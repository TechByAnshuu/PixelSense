How It Works

From upload to insight in seconds

A fully serverless, event-driven pipeline processes your images with zero server management on your end.

01
🔐

Authenticate Securely

Sign in via Amazon Cognito. Every request is verified with a scoped JWT — your identity is always protected.

02
☁️

Upload Your Image

PixelSense issues a time-limited presigned S3 URL. Your image travels directly to secure cloud storage — our servers never touch the raw bytes.

03
🤖

AI Analysis Fires

An SQS-triggered Lambda calls Rekognition for object detection, face analysis, celebrity recognition & OCR, then Bedrock synthesizes a plain-language summary.

04
📊

View Your Results

Results land in DynamoDB keyed to your user. The app polls until complete, then displays your full analysis history — always yours, always private.

Browser→ Cognito JWT→ API Gateway→ Lambda→ S3 Upload→ SQS Event→ Rekognition+ Bedrock AI→ DynamoDB
Capabilities

Four AI engines.
One platform.

Every image you upload runs through four distinct AI pipelines, returning structured JSON data plus a human-readable summary.

🏷️

Object Detection

Rekognition DetectLabels identifies thousands of objects, scenes, and concepts with confidence scores. Get structured label hierarchies in milliseconds.

👤

Face & Celebrity Recognition

Detect faces, estimate attributes (age range, emotion, eyewear), and identify public figures via RecognizeCelebrities — all in a single pass.

📝

OCR Text Extraction

DetectText surfaces printed and handwritten text from signs, receipts, documents, and product labels — returning bounding boxes and confidence scores.

✨

Bedrock AI Summary

All Rekognition outputs are fed as structured JSON context to Amazon Bedrock, which generates a concise 2-4 sentence plain-language description of your image. Perfect for accessibility, content tagging, and search indexing.

Rekognition JSON → Bedrock Prompt → Plain English Summary

🔄

Resilient by Design

SQS decouples uploads from processing. DLQ catches failures after 3 retries. CloudWatch alarms alert on DLQ depth. Your images are never lost.

🛡️

Zero-Trust Security

Presigned S3 URLs expire in 5 minutes. All API routes require a valid Cognito JWT. Lambda IAM roles follow least-privilege. Throttling caps cost exposure.
